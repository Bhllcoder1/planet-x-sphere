Notice
------

The CADC data collections are an open data source for the international astronomy community, provided by the National Research Council of Canada. While publicly available, the CADC data collections, products and services are a digital research facility for professional astronomers and are not intended for general public use.

The CADC uses digital markers (cookies) on portions of their websites
---------------------------------------------------------------------

During your on-line visit, your browser exchanges data with CADC's web servers. You may adjust your browser to reject digital markers, including cookies, if you so choose. However, this will restrict the features of CADC websites that your browser can utilize.
